Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

How can I report a calculator issue?
Email us at the Website Issues address above, or use the contact form and select "Website Issue" as the subject. Include which calculator you were using and what went wrong so we can fix it quickly.
How do I suggest a new tool?
We love new ideas. Send a message through the form above with "Calculator Feedback" selected, and describe the calculator you'd like to see.
Can I request a feature?
Yes. Feature requests for existing calculators, like adding tax brackets or currency options, can be submitted the same way. Just describe what you need and why.
Are the calculators free?
Every calculator on Small Business Calc is completely free, with no sign-up, subscription, or hidden paywall.
Do you offer business consulting?
We focus on building self-serve calculators rather than offering one-on-one consulting. If your business inquiry is about a partnership, use the Business Inquiries category above.
How quickly do you respond?
We aim to respond to all messages within 2–3 business days. More technical issues may take a little longer to investigate properly.
