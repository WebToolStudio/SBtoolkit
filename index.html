FAQ

Common Questions About These Calculators

Answers to the questions small business owners ask most before using these tools.

A profit margin calculator helps business owners determine what percentage of their revenue they keep as profit after deducting costs. Enter your cost price and selling price to instantly see your gross profit, profit margin percentage, and markup.
Break-even revenue is calculated by dividing your total fixed costs by your contribution margin ratio. The contribution margin is your selling price minus variable costs per unit. Our break-even calculator handles this automatically — enter your fixed costs, variable cost per unit, and price per unit.
Profitable pricing starts with knowing your total costs, including labor, materials, overhead, and desired profit. Use our Service Pricing Calculator to set a price that covers all costs and delivers your target margin. A common mistake is pricing based on competitors without knowing your own cost structure.
Total employee cost goes well beyond the salary. You need to include employer payroll taxes (Social Security, Medicare), health insurance contributions, workers' compensation, paid time off, equipment and software, and any training costs. Our Employee Cost Calculator estimates the true annual cost of a new hire.
A hiring ROI calculator helps you determine whether a new hire will generate enough revenue or savings to justify their total cost. It compares the expected revenue or productivity contribution of the employee against their full annual cost to give you a return on investment percentage.
Project profitability is estimated by subtracting all project-related costs — labor hours, materials, subcontractors, overhead allocation — from the total project revenue. Our Project Profitability Estimator walks you through each cost category and shows your profit margin and net profit per project.
