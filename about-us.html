About Small Business Calc

Helping small business owners make smarter financial and operational decisions through simple, free, and practical business tools.

Our Mission

Running a small business means making pricing and money decisions constantly, often without the time, budget, or background to get them exactly right. We built this site because the same few questions come up again and again for owners, freelancers, and contractors:

  • Pricing services correctly
  • Understanding profit margins
  • Managing payroll expenses
  • Evaluating hiring decisions
  • Planning revenue growth
  • Improving profitability

Small Business Calc exists to take the guesswork out of those questions. Every calculator on this site is built to turn a messy, manual calculation into a clear answer in under a minute, so you can make better decisions and spend less time wrestling with formulas.

Who These Tools Are For

Small Business Owners

Run the numbers behind pricing, payroll, and profit so your business stays sustainable as it grows.

Set hourly rates and project prices that actually cover your costs, taxes, and time off.

Entrepreneurs

Frequently Asked Questions

Why was this website created?
Small Business Calc was created because too many small business owners price their work, hire staff, or plan growth using guesswork instead of numbers. We built a set of free, focused calculators so anyone can run the math behind a pricing or staffing decision in a couple of minutes, without needing a spreadsheet or an accountant on call.
Are the calculators free?
Yes. Every calculator on this site is free to use, with no paywalls, trial limits, or hidden tiers.
Who can use these tools?
Anyone making pricing or operational decisions for a business can use these tools, including small business owners, freelancers, contractors, consultants, service providers, and entrepreneurs.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no registration, login, or account required. Open a calculator, enter your numbers, and get your results immediately.
Are calculations guaranteed?
No. Every result is an estimate based on the numbers you enter. These tools are educational and informational, not legal, accounting, tax, or financial advice, so you should confirm important decisions with a qualified professional.
Can freelancers use these tools?
Yes. Several calculators, including pricing, hourly rate, and invoicing tools, are built specifically with freelancers and independent contractors in mind.
