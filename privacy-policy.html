Introduction

Your privacy matters to us. Small Business Calc ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to being transparent about how this website operates and what happens to information when you visit it or use any of our free business calculators.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you. It applies to every page and tool hosted under the Small Business Calc website.

This policy is effective as of June 20, 2026. By continuing to use this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little information as possible while still being able to operate and improve the website. Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Non-personal usage information, such as the pages you visit, how long you spend on them, and which calculators you use.
  • Analytics information, including aggregated and anonymized statistics about traffic and on-site behavior.
  • Browser and device information, such as browser type and version, operating system, screen size, and approximate location at a country or city level.
  • Contact form submissions, including any name, email address, or message content you choose to provide when reaching out to us.
The numbers you type into our calculators (cost, price, hours, rates, etc.) are processed entirely in your browser and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

How Information Is Used

Any information we do collect is used only for legitimate purposes related to operating and improving this website, including to:

  • Improve website performance, speed, and reliability.
  • Analyze traffic patterns and understand which tools are most useful.
  • Enhance the overall user experience and usability of our calculators.
  • Respond to questions, support requests, or other inquiries you send us.
  • Protect the website's security and detect or prevent fraud, abuse, or technical issues.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your browser. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as preferences or repeat visits.

  • We and our partners may use cookies to remember basic preferences, measure traffic, and, where applicable, serve relevant advertising.
  • Most browsers let you view, manage, block, or delete cookies through their settings menu.
  • You can choose to disable cookies at any time, though some parts of the site may not function exactly as intended without them.

Google Analytics

This website may use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to help us understand how visitors use the site.

  • Google Analytics collects anonymous, aggregated data — it does not identify you personally.
  • This data helps us measure overall traffic and which pages and calculators are visited most.
  • We use these insights solely to improve site performance and content.

If you'd prefer not to be measured by Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

This website may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising networks.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites.
  • These advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads that may be more relevant to your interests.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

For more detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's Advertising Policies.
